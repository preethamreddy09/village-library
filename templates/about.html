About the Village Reading Room

What we're trying to do

Every book here is free to borrow. There's no membership fee and no paperwork beyond a name and a due date — the only thing we ask is that books come back so the next person can read them too.

Behind the scenes, every loan we record also helps us understand what the village actually wants to read — which genres and books people keep coming back for — so future additions to the shelves are guided by real interest, not guesswork.

Everyone is welcome

Whether you're here to borrow a book, suggest one, or just see what we're building — you're welcome to browse the catalog or see what the village is reading.
